Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This policy explains what information Expense Tracker ("the app") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The app is built and operated by Atreya SoftTech. By creating an account you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (your password is handled entirely by our authentication provider, Neon Auth — we never see or store it ourselves).
  • Expense records: the date, amount, category, and any note you enter or that is extracted from a scanned bill.
  • Bill images/PDFs you scan: processed to extract expense details (see below). We do not keep a permanent copy of the image itself after the scan request completes.
  • Usage data: the number of AI scans you make and their approximate processing cost, used to enforce your monthly scan allowance and for our own cost tracking.

How We Use AI to Scan Bills

When you use "Scan Bill with AI", the photo or PDF you provide is sent to Google's Gemini API to extract the amount, date, category, and merchant name. This means the content of any bill you scan — including anything visible on it — is transmitted to Google for processing under Google's API terms. Only scan genuine purchase bills; avoid scanning documents that contain sensitive information you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

How We Store Your Data

Expense records and account status are stored in a Neon PostgreSQL database. Authentication (sign-up, sign-in, password resets) is handled by Neon Auth. Your session token is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Who Can See Your Data

Your expense records are private to your account — no other user, including other approved users of this app, can view, edit, or delete them. The app administrator can see your email address, account status (approved / pending / deactivated), and your AI scan count and cost, but cannot see your individual expense records. New accounts require one-time administrator approval before use.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers necessary to run the app:

  • Google (Gemini API) — to process bills you choose to scan.
  • Neon — our database and authentication provider.
  • Render — our application hosting provider.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your expense records are retained until you delete them individually or ask us to delete your account. To request full account and data deletion, contact the administrator or reach out via atreyasofttech.in.

Your Rights

You may access, edit, or delete your own expense records at any time within the app. You may request a full export of your data (via the Export to Excel feature) or request account deletion at any time.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to Atreya SoftTech.
